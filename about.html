About S&R

S&R is Sam & Ryan — two brothers who never grew out of the thrill of the pull.

A shop built around the excitement of the find

We started the way every collector starts: a binder, a shoebox, a stack of comics that was definitely not allowed in the house. Somewhere along the way the hobby became the mission — a shop where the hunt never ends and every aisle can surprise you.

Our philosophy is Curated Chaos: a packed, exciting store where you never know what you'll discover next — but everything in it earned its spot. No junk, no fakes, no mysteries.

🖐️ Hand-Curated

Every find is picked by Sam & Ryan personally. If it wouldn't go in our own collection, it doesn't go in the vault.

❤️ Collector-First

Honest grades, honest prices, honest descriptions. Beginners get the Budget Bin; veterans get the grails. Everybody gets the truth.

🧭 Built to Discover

The vault rewards the curious. Dig through the bins, chase the categories, stumble onto the thing you didn't know you needed.

Where this is going
