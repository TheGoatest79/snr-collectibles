🤝 Sell With Us

Your grails, our audience. List your own finds in the vault — or hand them to Sam & Ryan and let us do the selling.

Two ways to sell

📋 List it yourself

Up to 4 items at a time, per seller. The cap keeps the vault curated — quality over quantity. Snap your photos, write an honest description, set your price, and your finds go live for every collector browsing the shop.

One flat 9% fee when it sells. No monthly subscription, no listing fees — list free, we take 9% only when your item sells. (eBay takes ~13%. Just saying.)

Seller accounts are open — sign up below and list your first item in minutes.

📦 Consignment

Don't want the hassle? Ship your items to S&R and we sell them for you — professional photos, honest grading with our AI-assisted grader, listing, packing, and shipping handled start to finish. You get paid when it sells, minus an agreed consignment cut.

Consignment terms and revenue split will be posted here before we go live.

How listing works

Make your profile

Sign up free and create your seller profile — your shop name, a blurb, your avatar. Like eBay: you're selling in minutes, not waiting on approval.

List (max 4)

Photograph your items, describe condition honestly, set your price. Four live listings at a time keeps things curated.

Get graded

Run your cards, comics, or coins through our AI grading assistant for a pre-grade estimate buyers can trust — sellers get one flat rate.

Sell & ship

Buyers check out securely. You pack and ship — or upgrade any sale to consignment and we handle it. We take a flat 9% when it sells. That's it.
